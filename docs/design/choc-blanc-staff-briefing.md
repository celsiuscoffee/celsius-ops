# Choc Blanc — Staff Briefing (WhatsApp)

Send to all three outlet groups on **30 Aug 2026**, before the SMS goes out.
Built on the four campaign phases in `choc-blanc-merdeka-campaign.md`.

Written to be read on a phone, standing up, before a shift. Every line is
either a fact staff need at the till or a sentence they can say out loud.
Anything that was explanation rather than instruction has been cut.

**The phase dates are send windows, not voucher lifetimes.** Because the SMS
drips over five days and each voucher runs 7 days from issue, Phase 1 vouchers
stay live into Phase 2 and Phase 3 vouchers into Phase 4. The brief leads with
that rule — without it, staff refuse a valid voucher in week two.

Mont Blanc STAYS. Both on the menu, same price.

## Notes for whoever sends this

- **Send on 30 Aug, not the 31st** — the SMS goes out on Merdeka eve, so the
  first B1F1 customer can walk in the moment doors open.
- **The golden rule is the load-bearing line.** The drip plus 7-day validity
  means live vouchers outlast their phase; without it a customer gets refused
  in week two holding something valid.
- **"It goes out in batches" is the other line staff get asked for.** There is
  genuinely no list to check.
- **Round A holders have no voucher.** The 526 known Mont Blanc buyers get an
  announcement only — they will hear about Choc Blanc and arrive with nothing
  in their account. That is correct, not a fault. Staff need no special
  handling for it: the golden rule already covers them ("nothing in the
  account" → full price), which is why the brief doesn't mention rounds at all.
- If the voucher arm is dropped, delete THE VOUCHER block and change the two
  B1F1 weeks to "no vouchers" — never leave staff expecting vouchers that were
  not issued.

# CC-HR-012 — Salary Bands & Pay Review

## 1. Purpose

Sets the pay range for each position so that two people doing the same job are
paid comparably, an offer can be made without a case-by-case negotiation, and
a figure outside the range is a visible decision rather than an accident.

## 2. Scope

Basic salary for all full-time positions, and the hourly rate for part-time
and casual engagements, at every Celsius Coffee outlet and at HQ.

Not covered: allowances (CC-HR-003 §5.1), overtime rates, or the performance
standards that gate the performance allowance (CC-HR-007).

## 3. Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Band | The minimum and maximum basic salary for a position |
| Floor | The statutory minimum wage; never breached, regardless of band |
| Out-of-band | A basic salary outside the published range for the position |
| Red-circled | An out-of-band salary that is approved and preserved, not corrected downward |

## 4. Roles & responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility in this document |
|---|---|
| Managing Director | Sets and reviews bands; approves every out-of-band salary in writing |
| Head of Operations | Applies bands when drafting offers; reports out-of-band cases at review |
| Outlet manager | Raises pay proposals through the Head of Operations, never directly to the employee |

## 5. Procedure

### 5.1 Bands

1. The bands are:

   | Position | Band (RM/month) |
   |---|---|
   | Barista | 1,700 – 2,300 |
   | Kitchen Crew | 1,700 – 2,200 |
   | Shift Lead | 2,000 – 2,500 |
   | Kitchen Lead | 2,100 – 2,700 |
   | Barista Lead | 2,500 – 3,500 |
   | Manager | 3,000 – 4,000 |
   | Head of Department | No band — set individually by the Managing Director |
   | Director | No band — not salaried through this policy |

   | Engagement | Band (RM/hour) |
   |---|---|
   | Part-time, weekday | 9.00 – 11.00 |
   | Part-time, weekend | May be set per person, at or above the weekday rate |
   | Casual | 9.00 – 11.00 |

2. **Floor.** No basic salary is set below the statutory minimum wage
   (RM1,700/month at the date of this draft), whatever the band says and
   whatever the employee agrees to. A term purporting to pay less is void to
   that extent and the statutory figure applies.

3. Bands are a range, not an entitlement. Position in the band reflects
   experience and the standards met under CC-HR-007; it is not automatic and
   does not rise with length of service alone.

### 5.2 Out-of-band salaries

4. An out-of-band figure may be offered or retained only with the Managing
   Director's written approval, recorded with the instrument that sets it.
5. An existing salary above band is **red-circled**: it is preserved, not
   reduced. Reducing pay to fit a band introduced after the fact is a
   unilateral variation of contract and is not done.
6. An existing salary below band is corrected upward at the next review, or
   sooner if it is below the floor, in which case it is corrected immediately.

### 5.3 System enforcement

7. The system **blocks** a basic salary below the statutory floor. This is a
   hard stop, not a warning.
8. The system **warns** when a basic salary falls outside the band for the
   position, and records the warning being overridden together with who
   overrode it. A warning is not a block: an approved out-of-band figure must
   still be enterable.
9. Positions with no band (Head of Department, Director) are exempt from the
   warning but not from the floor.

### 5.4 Review

10. Bands are reviewed annually by the Managing Director, and additionally
    within 30 days of any change to the statutory minimum wage.
11. Each review records: the bands as they stood, every out-of-band case and
    whether it remains approved, and the bands as revised.

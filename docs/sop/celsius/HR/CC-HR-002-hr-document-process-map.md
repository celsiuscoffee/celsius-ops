# CC-HR-002 — HR Document Process Map

## 1. Purpose

Tells a manager which HR document to use for the situation in front of them,
in what order, and by when — so that the right instrument is issued the first
time and nothing that affects someone's pay or job status happens on a verbal
agreement alone.

This is an index and a routing document. It never restates the content of the
documents it points to.

## 2. Scope

All Celsius Coffee employment instruments: offers, contracts, schedules,
handbook, onboarding, and the employment letter series (CC-L00 – CC-L24).
Applies to full-time, part-time and casual engagements at every outlet.

Not covered: the mechanics of onboarding a hire into the system
(CC-HR-001), payroll processing, or salary setting.

## 3. Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Instrument | A document issued to one named person that creates, changes or ends an employment relationship or records a decision about it |
| Template | The controlled master a given instrument is produced from; has a `CC-HR-0nn` ID in the registry |
| Reference number | The serial carried by an issued instrument, e.g. `CC-OFR-2026-001`; identifies that copy, not the template |
| Letter series | CC-L00 – CC-L24, the numbered employment letters indexed by CC-HR-010 |
| Order of precedence | Which document wins when two disagree (§5.4) |

## 4. Roles & responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility in this document |
|---|---|
| Managing Director | Approves and signs offers, contracts, confirmations and terminations; approves any term outside the published bands |
| Head of Operations | Owns this map; issues instruments and allocates reference numbers; keeps the issued-instrument register |
| Outlet manager | Identifies the situation, requests the right instrument, collects signatures, briefs the employee |
| Employee | Signs and returns; receives their copy |

## 5. Procedure

### 5.1 Which document, for which situation

| Situation | Issue | Template | When |
|---|---|---|---|
| Making a job offer | Letter of Offer | CC-HR-003 | Before any start date is agreed |
| Offer accepted, full-time | Employment Contract — Full-Time | CC-HR-004 | At offer acceptance, before day one |
| Offer accepted, part-time | Employment Contract — Part-Time | CC-HR-005 | At offer acceptance, before day one |
| Irregular, on-call engagement | Engagement Terms — Casual | CC-HR-006 | Before the first shift |
| Day one | Performance Standards Schedule | CC-HR-007 | Signed on day one, by position |
| Day one | Staff Handbook acknowledgment | CC-HR-008 | Acknowledged on day one |
| Day one | Onboarding Pack | CC-HR-009 | Collected on day one |
| Probation passed | Confirmation of Employment (CC-L01) | CC-HR-010 | Within 7 days of probation end |
| Probation not yet passed | Probation Extension (CC-L02) | CC-HR-010 | Before probation end, never after |
| Conduct or performance issue | Warning series (CC-L10 – CC-L13) | CC-HR-010 | Per CC-HR-011 |
| Misconduct requiring inquiry | Domestic Inquiry series (CC-L15 – CC-L18) | CC-HR-010 | Per CC-HR-011 |
| Position or salary change | Variation of Terms (CC-L05) | CC-HR-010 | Before the change takes effect |
| Employee resigns | Acceptance of Resignation (CC-L20) | CC-HR-010 | Within 3 working days |
| Employment ends | Termination / Release (CC-L21 – CC-L23) | CC-HR-010 | On or before the last day |
| Employee requests proof of employment | Employment Verification (CC-L24) | CC-HR-010 | On request |

### 5.2 Reference numbers

1. Every issued instrument carries a reference number. The format is
   `CC-{TYPE}-{YYYY}-{nnn}`, where `TYPE` identifies the instrument
   (`OFR` offer, `CON` contract, `LTR` letter) and `nnn` is sequential within
   that type and year, starting at `001` and **never reused**.
2. The year is the year of issue, not the year the employment starts.
3. Reference numbers are allocated by the Head of Operations and recorded in
   the issued-instrument register (§6) at the moment of allocation — including
   for an instrument that is later withdrawn or declined, so the series never
   has a silent gap.
4. A reissued instrument that corrects an error takes a **new** number and
   states the number it supersedes. The superseded copy is retained.

### 5.3 Signature and retention

5. No instrument takes effect until signed by both the Managing Director (or
   delegate) and the employee, except letters that record a unilateral company
   decision, which take effect on issue and acknowledge receipt only.
6. The signed copy is filed to the employee's documents in the private HR store
   (Backoffice → HR → Employees). The employee gets a copy at signing.
7. An unsigned instrument is never the basis for starting work, changing pay,
   or ending employment.

### 5.4 Order of precedence

8. Where documents disagree, the order is:
   **Statutory rights → Letter of Offer / Contract → Performance Standards
   Schedule → Staff Handbook → this map.**
9. Statutory rights under the Employment Act 1955 and its regulations override
   every document in this manual. Where a document appears to give less than
   the statutory minimum, the statutory minimum applies and the document is
   raised to its owner as a defect.

### 5.5 Timing

10. An instrument that must be issued "before" an event has no effect if issued
    after it. A probation extension issued after probation has lapsed does not
    extend probation; the employee is confirmed by operation of the contract.
11. Where this map gives a deadline in working days, the clock starts the first
    working day after the triggering event.

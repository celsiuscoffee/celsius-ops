# CC-HR-003 — Letter of Offer

## 1. Purpose

Defines the one instrument that creates an employment relationship at Celsius
Coffee: what it must contain, who signs it, and what must never be left blank.
An offer made any other way — verbally, over WhatsApp, in a roster — is not an
offer and does not bind the company or the candidate.

## 2. Scope

Every engagement at every outlet: full-time, part-time and casual. Issued once
per candidate per offer; a changed offer is a new letter with a new reference
(CC-HR-002 §5.2).

Not covered: internal moves and pay changes for existing staff, which take a
Variation of Terms (CC-L05), not a new offer.

## 3. Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Offer | The company's written proposal of employment on stated terms |
| Acceptance | The candidate's signature on the acknowledgement, returned to the company |
| Lapse | An offer not accepted by its stated deadline ceases to be open |
| Conditional term | A term that only takes effect on a future event, e.g. an allowance payable after confirmation |

## 4. Roles & responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility in this document |
|---|---|
| Managing Director | Approves the terms and signs the letter |
| Head of Operations | Drafts, allocates the `CC-OFR-{YYYY}-{nnn}` reference, issues, and files the signed copy |
| Outlet manager | Requests the hire; briefs the candidate on the role before the letter is issued |
| Candidate | Signs and returns by the stated deadline; brings the Onboarding Pack items on day one |

## 5. Procedure

### 5.1 Mandatory content

1. Every Letter of Offer carries, without exception:

   | Field | Rule |
   |---|---|
   | Reference number | `CC-OFR-{YYYY}-{nnn}`, allocated before issue |
   | Date of letter | Date of issue |
   | Full name | Exactly as on the NRIC or passport |
   | NRIC / passport number | As on the document; used for payroll and statutory registration |
   | Position | A position named in CC-HR-012 §5.1, or the letter needs MD approval of a new one |
   | Employment type | Full-time, part-time or casual |
   | Commencement date | The first working day |
   | Place of work | The named outlet, plus the mobility clause |
   | Remuneration | Monthly basic for full-time; hourly rate for part-time and casual |
   | Allowances | Each allowance, its amount, and the condition attached to it |
   | Probation | Length, and that it is extendable once (full-time only) |
   | Notice period | Per §5.3 |
   | Acceptance deadline | A date, after which the offer lapses |

2. **No field in that table may be issued blank, as "TBD", or as a
   placeholder.** An offer with an unfilled rate is not an offer; it is a
   negotiation the candidate cannot evaluate and the company cannot enforce.

3. A conditional term states its condition in the same sentence as its amount.
   "RM200 allowance after confirmation" is complete; "RM200 allowance" with the
   condition only in a later clause is not.

### 5.2 Terms — current standard

4. Unless the Managing Director approves otherwise in writing, the standard
   terms are:

   | Term | Full-time | Part-time | Casual |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | Probation | 3 months, extendable once | None | None |
   | Notice, during probation | 2 weeks | — | — |
   | Notice, after confirmation | 2 calendar months | 1 week | 1 week |
   | Pay cycle | Monthly, on or before the 7th | Weekly | Weekly |
   | Basis of pay | Monthly basic | Hourly, on recorded clock-in/out | Hourly, on recorded clock-in/out |
   | Statutory | EPF, SOCSO, EIS, PCB where applicable | Same | Same |
   | Annual leave | Per Employment Act 1955 s.60E | Pro-rated, per the Part-Time Regulations 2010 | Pro-rated |

5. Probation may be extended **once**, by written notice issued **before** the
   probation end date, stating the standards not yet met (CC-L02). An extension
   issued late has no effect (CC-HR-002 §5.5).

6. The basic salary must sit within the band for the position (CC-HR-012). A
   figure below the statutory minimum wage is never issued. A figure outside
   the band requires the Managing Director's approval, recorded with the offer.

### 5.3 Issue and acceptance

7. The letter is signed by the Managing Director before issue. An unsigned
   letter is a draft and is not sent.
8. The candidate signs the acknowledgement and returns it by the deadline. The
   signed copy is filed per CC-HR-002 §5.3 and is the trigger for creating the
   system account (CC-HR-001 §5.1).
9. An offer that lapses is marked lapsed in the issued-instrument register. Its
   reference number is not reused.
10. An offer withdrawn before acceptance is withdrawn in writing, stating the
    date of withdrawal. Withdrawal after acceptance is a termination, not a
    withdrawal, and follows the letter series.

### 5.4 Backdating

11. A commencement date earlier than the date of the letter is permitted only
    where the employee has genuinely been working since that date, and the
    Managing Director approves it in writing. Backdating pulls EPF, SOCSO and
    EIS liability back to the commencement date for every intervening month;
    that cost is confirmed with Finance before the letter is issued.

# CC-HR-001 — New Hire Onboarding

## 1. Purpose

Gets a new staff member from signed offer to productive, compliant, and paid
correctly — with a system account, the right access, the required
certifications recorded, and every onboarding step ticked in the system so
nothing depends on memory.

## 2. Scope

All new hires at all Celsius Coffee outlets: full-time, part-time, and
managers. Not covered: salary setting/negotiation (Managing Director),
resignation/offboarding (separate document), payroll processing.

## 3. Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| LoE | Letter of Employment (offer letter), the hire's contractual record |
| HOO | Head of Operations — confirmer for staff-record changes in the HR authority matrix |
| Stations | The house area(s) a role works: FOH (bar/counter), BOH (kitchen), lead |
| Onboarding checklist | The per-hire task list in Backoffice → HR, tracked to completion |

## 4. Roles & responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Managing Director | Approves the hire and the salary; confirms any later salary/bank change (two-person rule) |
| Head of Operations | Confirms staff-record creation and changes; owns this procedure |
| Outlet manager | Runs day one at the outlet; briefs required SOPs; completes checklist items |
| New hire | Completes documents and certifications; acknowledges required SOPs |

## 5. Procedure

### 5.1 Before day one

1. **Offer letter signed.** The signed LoE is the trigger for everything
   below. File the PDF into the employee's documents (private HR store) —
   the LoE import tool can create the staff record directly from the PDF.
2. **Create the system account** (one of: Backoffice HR → employees, the HR
   WhatsApp agent, or LoE import). Creation follows the HR authority
   matrix: a manager may request, the **HOO confirms** (Managing Director
   and HOO confirm their own). The record must carry: full name, IC number,
   phone, outlet, position, employment type, start date, salary, bank
   details.
3. The system derives from the position: **stations** (barista/cashier →
   FOH, kitchen → BOH, leads add lead) and the **standard access preset**
   for the role. Do not hand-grant extra apps or modules at hire — access
   beyond the preset is a separate, justified request.
4. **Set the login PIN: 6 digits.** (The system accepts 4–6, but native app
   logins require 6 — a shorter PIN locks the hire out of the POS/apps.)
   The hire changes it to a private PIN on first login. PINs are personal
   and never shared — see CC-FIN-001 §12 on manager PINs.
5. Schedule the hire's first shifts in the roster so day-one clock-in and
   POS login work (unscheduled staff need a manager override to open the
   till).

### 5.2 Day one at the outlet

6. Outlet manager opens the hire's **onboarding checklist** in Backoffice →
   HR and works through it; every completed item is ticked in the system
   (the employee list shows progress until green). Un-ticked items are the
   manager's open work, not the hire's.
7. Walk the hire through: geofenced **clock in/out** on the staff app,
   their checklists (auto-assigned by station), where SOPs live, and how
   claims and leave are requested.
8. **Brief and collect acknowledgment** for the required document set for
   the hire's stations (per CC-GOV-001 §5.5): at minimum CC-FIN-001
   Payments & Refunds (FOH) and CC-OPS-001 Incident & Complaint Handling
   (everyone). Interim record: signed sheet or Google Form.

### 5.3 First month — compliance

9. **Food handler certificate (Sijil Pengendali Makanan)** is mandatory for
   all outlet staff: record it in HR → certifications **with its expiry
   date** on day one if held, otherwise book the course in week one and
   record on completion. Other certs (halal awareness, first aid, fire
   safety, barista) are recorded the same way as obtained.
10. Health requirements not yet tracked as their own type (e.g. typhoid
    injection) are recorded as a certification of type *other* until the
    system gains a dedicated type.
11. Statutory registrations (EPF/SOCSO/EIS enrolment for the new hire)
    are completed before the first payroll run.

### 5.4 Probation

12. Probation length is per the LoE. Before it ends, the outlet manager
    submits the **probation review** in the system; the outcome is confirm,
    extend (a second review row — both kept), or not confirmed.
13. On confirmation, generate the **confirmation letter** from the system
    and file it to the employee's documents.

### 5.5 Later changes (for reference)

14. Any later **salary or bank-detail change** follows the two-person rule:
    requested at HOO level or above, **confirmed by the Managing Director**
    (only the MD may act alone).

## 6. Records

| Record | Where | Retention |
|---|---|---|
| Signed LoE, confirmation letter | HR documents (private bucket) | Duration of employment + 7 years |
| Onboarding checklist progress | Backoffice HR (per-hire, per-task, who/when) | Duration of employment |
| Certifications with expiry | HR → certifications | Duration of employment |
| Probation reviews | HR probation records (all rows kept) | Duration of employment + 7 years |
| SOP acknowledgments | Interim sign-sheet/form; system from SOP-module phase 1 | 2 years after superseded |

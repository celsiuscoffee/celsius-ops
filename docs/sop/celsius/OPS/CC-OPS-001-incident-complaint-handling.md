# CC-OPS-001 — Incident & Complaint Handling

## 1. Purpose

Turns an unhappy customer into a recovered one, fast and at the right level:
frontline fixes what can be fixed on the spot, managers own compensation and
public replies, and dangerous cases go straight to the top. Also defines the
interim record for physical incidents (injury, safety, property) until a
dedicated incident log exists.

## 2. Scope

All Celsius Coffee outlets and all complaint channels: in person at the
counter, the in-outlet QR feedback form, Google reviews, and messages
arriving via delivery platforms or social media. Applies to every staff
member. Not covered: staff-vs-staff grievances (HR disciplinary process),
supplier disputes (PROC).

## 3. Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| QR feedback | The in-outlet rating form; ratings below the threshold (default 4★) come to us internally instead of Google |
| Recovery case | A negative review/feedback being worked toward a compensated, resolved outcome |
| Recovery voucher | Single-use free-coffee voucher issued via a recovery code, one per customer |
| Escalation case | A complaint containing a danger trigger (§5.4) — owner-level only |

## 4. Roles & responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| All staff (FOH/BOH) | Immediate response at the counter; remake/replace on the spot; report incidents same shift |
| Outlet manager | Owns every case at their outlet: responds to nudges, decides compensation, records outcomes |
| Area Manager | Recovery owner: approves negative-review replies, chases the backlog, decides review penalties |
| Managing Director | Escalation cases; final word on refunds (per CC-FIN-001) |

## 5. Procedure

### 5.1 In person, at the counter (any staff, no approval needed)

1. Listen fully, apologise once, sincerely. Do not argue, do not explain
   internal reasons.
2. **Fix what you can immediately: remake or replace the item, free.** Every
   staff member has this authority without asking — a remade drink is the
   cheapest recovery we will ever buy.
3. If the customer wants more than a remake (compensation, refund, or is
   still angry): hand to the shift's senior/manager **now**, in person if on
   site, by phone if not. Never promise money — refunds are governed by
   CC-FIN-001 §5.4.
4. Tell the manager what happened before your shift ends, even if the
   customer left satisfied.

### 5.2 System-detected cases (QR feedback & Google reviews)

5. Low QR ratings and negative Google reviews reach the outlet automatically:
   on-shift staff and managers get a WhatsApp nudge within minutes. **The
   nudge is a task, not information** — the outlet manager takes the case
   the same shift.
6. If the customer's phone number is known (QR feedback), the manager calls
   or messages the customer personally, then records the outcome on the
   case: compensated (voucher issued), resolved, or dismissed.
7. **Replies to negative Google reviews are never auto-posted.** The Area
   Manager (or Managing Director) reviews the drafted reply in the
   backoffice board and approves, edits, or rejects it. Approved replies
   may carry a single-use recovery code; the customer redeems it themselves
   and gets the recovery voucher.
8. No case sits: drafts are actioned within **72 hours**; the daily backlog
   chaser lists anything older, and clearing that list is the Area
   Manager's job.
9. Never put a personal phone number in a public reply.

### 5.3 Physical incidents (interim procedure)

10. Injury to a customer or staff, food-safety suspicion, property damage,
    or theft: make the scene safe first, get medical help if needed, then
    the outlet manager reports to the Area Manager by phone **the same
    hour** — not text, not next day.
11. The same day, the manager writes the record: what happened, when, who
    was involved, photos, action taken — sent to the Area Manager and filed
    with the case. (A dedicated incident log in the system is planned; until
    then this written record is the register.)

### 5.4 Escalation cases — straight to the top

12. Any complaint mentioning: **food poisoning or health harm, injury,
    legal action or lawyers, discrimination, a refund demand with a threat,
    or severe public anger** — stop. Frontline and outlet managers do not
    reply, publicly or privately.
13. Escalate to the Area Manager and Managing Director immediately; any
    public reply is drafted for their explicit approval before posting.

### 5.5 Accountability

14. Google reviews of ≤3★ enter the review-penalty queue. The manager
    **attributes or dismisses each one deliberately** — an attributed
    penalty deducts RM10 from the responsible staff's performance
    allowance. Every pending penalty is decided before month-end payroll;
    letting them lapse undecided is not an option.
15. Unacknowledged operational alerts escalate to the owner after 90
    minutes. Acknowledging fast and fixing slow is still better than
    silence — silence is what escalates.

## 6. Records

| Record | Where | Retention |
|---|---|---|
| QR feedback cases + outcomes | Internal feedback records (backoffice) | 2 years |
| Negative-review cases, replies, decisions | Review board (draft → decided, by whom) | Permanent |
| Recovery vouchers issued/redeemed | Loyalty system, tagged to the review | Permanent |
| Physical incident reports (interim) | Written record to Area Manager + HR file if staff involved | 7 years |
| Review penalties (attributed/dismissed) | HR review-penalty queue | Duration of employment |
